Privacy & Cookies

What LvL stores in your browser, and why.

Essential — always on

LvL sets one first-party session cookie when you sign in. It keeps you logged in as you move between pages and carries the token that protects forms against cross-site request forgery. It is HttpOnly, so scripts cannot read it. Without it you cannot stay signed in, so it is not something consent can switch off.

  • session — sign-in state and CSRF protection

Preferences — optional

If you allow it, LvL remembers a few interface choices in your browser's local storage. These never leave your device and are not sent to our servers. Decline and the app still works; it just forgets these between visits.

  • lvl_lang — your chosen language
  • autoplay_next_reels — whether clips continue automatically
  • notification_sounds_enabled — notification sound setting
  • sidebar-menu-open — whether the desktop menu is expanded
  • lvl_cookie_consent — your answer on this page, so we stop asking

What LvL does not use

LvL does not load analytics, advertising or third-party tracking scripts, and does not set cookies for those purposes. If that ever changes, this page and the consent notice will change with it, and the new category will be off until you turn it on.

Account data

The details you enter — name, username, email, birthday, profile information — and the content you publish are stored in our database so the product can work. Deleting your account from Settings removes your account record.
